Orientation for .ply / .splat / .splatv

.ply

Standard 3DGS file for SuperSplat, Blender add-ons, Unity and Unreal plugins.

.splat

Compact web-viewer format.

.splatv

Animated splats with motion, for the splaTV viewer. Starts from your current view.

recipe .json

The recipe itself. Small, editable, and rebuilds the same scene any time.

AI access

On this site the AI buttons work for everyone through a shared AI with a small free daily allowance per person. When it runs out, Copy prompt and Paste recipe keep working with any AI chat.

With your own key the AI buttons don't use the shared allowance. The key stays in this browser and is sent only to Google. Leave it empty to use the shared AI.

Advanced: your own Worker, owner pass

The Worker keeps your Gemini and Groq keys off this page. It needs a /splat route; the splat-studio-worker.js file has it ready to deploy, with setup steps at the top.

AI requests use your free Gemini tier first. If Gemini is busy or out of quota, the Worker falls back to Cloudflare Workers AI (which can see photos, maps and renders) when its AI binding is added, then to Groq for text. The address is saved in this browser only.

For the Worker's owner: skips the per-visitor limit in this browser.
